Applied Pharmacy & Supply Chain Case Studies

Health Supply Chain

Improving Medicine Availability Through Reliable AMC Stock Planning

Challenge

High patient demand across referral hospital and humanitarian clinic settings creates constant risk of sudden stock-outs or over-ordering slow-moving items when consumption shifts go untracked.

Actions

Calculated maximum Average Monthly Consumption (AMC) from dispensing logs, reconciled bin cards against eLMIS/LMIS balances, enforced strict FEFO/FIFO rotation, and submitted timely MSD and central-store requisitions.

Result

Sustained over 97% commodity management accuracy across monthly internal supportive supervisions while maintaining continuous buffer availability for essential and chronic-care medicines.

Quality & Patient Safety

Strengthening Cold-Chain and Storage-Room Compliance

Challenge

Temperature excursions, improper stacking, or unmonitored shelf life can compromise potency for vaccines, biologics, laboratory reagents, and essential pharmaceuticals.

Actions

Enforced routine refrigerator and storage-room temperature logging, inspected incoming MSD and vendor deliveries against hospital SOPs, and segregated near-expiry or temperature-sensitive stock.

Result

Protected product potency and shelf life across high-turnover hospital and NGO stores with zero preventable cold-chain losses and full audit readiness during internal supervisions.

Workforce Capacity

Onboarding & Mentoring Pharmacy Interns for Dispensing Accuracy

Challenge

Newly arrived pharmacy interns and junior staff often face steep transitions when moving from classroom theory to high-volume dispensing (~100 daily items) and strict ledger reconciliation.

Actions

Established structured bench-side supervision covering lawful prescription verification, complete medication labeling, patient counselling checklists, and daily stock-card updates.

Result

Mentored approximately 40 interns and junior personnel to date, strengthening team dispensing speed, documentation consistency, and adherence to patient safety standards.

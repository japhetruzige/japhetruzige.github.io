What I Do

Core clinical pharmacy, health commodity management, quality assurance, and capacity-building responsibilities.

Dispensing, Prescription Review & Counselling

  • Prescription Review & Dispensing: Verify lawful prescriptions, check drug strengths, quantities, and expiration dates, and compound extemporaneous preparations when ordered.
  • Patient Counselling: Educate patients on safe medication use, adherence, dosing schedules, and when to seek urgent medical care.

CTC / ART / PEP / PrEP Services

  • Chronic & Communicable Care Support: Support patient-centered care and therapeutic education across HIV/ART, TB, and other chronic or communicable disease workflows.
  • Prevention & Continuity: Support timely access to antiretroviral therapy and prevention commodities (including ART, PEP, and PrEP workflows) with accurate dispensing records.

Stock Control, AMC/LMIS & MSD Ordering

  • Supply Chain & Inventory Control: Track stock balances, calculate Average Monthly Consumption (AMC), compile LMIS/eLMIS reports, and place timely requisitions with the Medical Stores Department (MSD) and central stores.
  • Controlled Medicines & Waste Prevention: Enforce strict physical security, stock rotation (FEFO/FIFO), expiry checks, and monthly physical counts.

Cold Chain, Pharmacovigilance, IPC & Mentorship

  • Cold Chain & Storage Conditions: Monitor refrigerator and storage-room temperatures and inspect medicines, medical supplies, and chemicals for shelf-life protection.
  • Pharmacovigilance & IPC: Document adverse drug reaction/pharmacovigilance reports and maintain strict cleanliness and infection prevention standards.
  • Mentorship: Guide and mentor pharmacy interns and junior staff in practical dispensing, documentation, and inventory routines.
